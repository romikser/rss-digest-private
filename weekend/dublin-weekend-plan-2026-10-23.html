Dublin, with options.

Saturday 24 – bank-holiday Monday 26 October 2026. Music, film, art and food, with real alternatives when the weather or tickets change the plan. Friday's guide understated the bank holiday and missed Monday, the marathon and IFI Horrorthon; this replaces that thin version.

The call

Storm Boelo changes Saturday. Met Éireann's Dublin forecast says heavy morning rain, localised flooding and gusty winds; showers and very strong gusts tonight. Saturday 13–15°C. Sunday is blustery with showers, easing later, 10–12°C. Bank-holiday Monday is wet again, heavy rain possible, 15–16°C. Make Saturday an indoor/gallery/cinema day, use Sunday's later outdoor window, and give Monday an indoor fallback. Recheck active warnings before travelling.
If I had one Saturday

Photography → experimental music

Photo Museum Ireland's TALENTS 2026 (11–18, free) → early dinner → Haunted Dancehall at NCH from 14:30 (Kelela day; check tickets). Night alternative: Leftfield DJ set at Centre Point.

If I had one Sunday

Park, then dancing

Let the morning showers pass; Stokerland in St Patrick's Park, then choose 2manydjs / Kormac at Vicar Street or a smaller club night. Avoid marathon road closures.

If I had one Monday

Modern art → giant street theatre

IMMA is open on bank holidays 12–17:30; see Technologies of Peace. Weather permitting, Macnas Fiáin parade at 19:00. If it's pouring, choose IFI Horrorthon instead.

The boring details that change your plan

  • Monday 26 is the October public holiday. Bram Stoker Festival explicitly runs 23–26 October. Check each café/shop's holiday hours; don't assume normal Monday service.
  • Clocks go back one hour at 02:00 Sunday. TFI's holiday notice says Nitelink runs Friday/Saturday, not Sunday, and its Saturday-night departures follow the old-clock timetable. Seven-day 24-hour bus routes still run.
  • Dublin Marathon is Sunday 25. TFI lists diversions 03:00–21:00 on Sunday, and some Merrion Square closures through Monday 18:00. Walk or use live TFI routing; don't promise a clean cross-city taxi.
  • Monday Dublin Bus uses a Sunday timetable. Macnas parade adds evening diversions to routes 7, 7a, 13, 33 and 41 variants. Check TFI's full notice on the day.
  • Ticket honesty: these are researched event listings, not live booking confirmations. Check booking links for sell-outs. In particular Stitch and THUMPER / ear were listed sold out and are not recommended as available choices.
