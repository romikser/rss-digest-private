🎭 Dir. Kenji Tanigaki · 2025 · Streaming from Jul 7
Hong Kong-style martial arts spectacular — "relentless, blood-soaked ballet of choreography, dir. Tanigaki delivering a brutal, balletic extravaganza." Empire gave it 4/5: "tipping its hat to HK classics, this knows exactly what its audience wants." Remarkable 97% for the genre.
87% Backrooms
🎭 Dir. Kane Parsons · 2025 · Streaming from Jul 14
YouTube creator Kane Parsons' assured feature debut turns internet liminal space mythology into genuine horror. Critics: "startlingly assured… bends the Backrooms into a horror film as mesmerizing as it is terrifying." Not typical horror — closer to existential dread.
81% Jinsei ★
🎭 Dir. Suzuki · 2025 · Streaming from Jul 17
Japanese animated film — "a century in the life of a glum Japanese boy-band exile, something like a cross between Zelig and Billy Pilgrim." Made over 18 months by one director. Variety: "belongs right there alongside the Summer of Awe." Personal and strange in the best way.
