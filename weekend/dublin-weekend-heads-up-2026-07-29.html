🎬 Cinema This Weekend
MUBI Go ★ FREE TICKET Rosebush Pruning
🎭 Dir. Karim Aïnouz · 2026 · Vue Dublin (check MUBI app for time)
Your MUBI Go pick this week: wicked satire of class, fashion and privilege. Wealthy American siblings stew in a Catalonian villa. Stars Dakota Fanning, Callum Turner, Riley Keough. Screenplay by Efthimis Filippou (The Lobster, Dogtooth). Sharp and strange — use your free ticket.
IFI ★★ EVENT The Odyssey (70mm)
🎭 Dir. Christopher Nolan · 2026 · 172 min · Sat: 13:00 · 17:00 · 19:50 · Sun: 13:00 · 19:50 · 📍 IFI
Nolan's epic Homer adaptation, showing exclusively in 70mm at the IFI. Matt Damon as Odysseus, Anne Hathaway as Penelope, Tom Holland as Telemachus. If you're going to see this, here is the place — proper 70mm format makes it a genuine event. Book ahead.
IFI ★★ PICK The Sacrifice (4K Restoration)
🎭 Dir. Andrei Tarkovsky · 1986 · 149 min · Sat: 20:20 · Sun: 12:00 · 20:10 · 📍 IFI
Tarkovsky's final film, freshly restored in 4K. A man vows to sacrifice everything if God will spare the world from disaster. Bleak and transcendent — the companion to Nostalghia that's been at Lighthouse. If you're into cinema, this is the unmissable double.
IFI ★ NEW ISH
🎭 Dir. Imran Perretta · 2025 · 89 min · UK · Sat: 11:00 · 15:50 · Sun: 18:25 · 📍 IFI
Debut feature co-written by Enda Walsh. Two 12-year-old Muslim friends in Luton are pulled apart after a traumatic police stop-and-search. Shot in black and white. Critics are comparing it to Shane Meadows — that's a strong endorsement for a debut.
🍽️ New Openings & Worth Knowing
NEW · D8 ★ NEARBY The Libertine
📍 57 Clanbrassil St Lower (Leonard's Corner), Dublin 8 · Gastropub & Cocktail Lounge
Victorian bar at Leonard's Corner — five minutes from Cork Street — reborn as a proper neighbourhood gastropub. Chef Lee Meyler's menu is short and focused: grilled and fried plates, built for sharing. The neighbourhood has adopted it fast. Your closest new opening by far.
NEW ★★ TOP PICK Kaldero
📍 South King Street, Dublin 2 · Irish-Filipino · Michelin Guide 2026
Dublin's first Irish-Filipino restaurant — open-fire cooking, Filipino family recipes, Irish produce. Sisig, humba, grilled chicken in banana ketchup & 7Up. From the Bahay pop-up team. Calamansi and pandan cocktails. Nothing else in the city like it — very much in Space Jaru / Chimac territory.
NEW ★ PICK Hawker
📍 Rathgar Road, Dublin 6 · Modern Chinese Street Food · From Hang Dai team
The Hang Dai team's second act in Rathmines: cheeseburger spring rolls with Sichuan ketchup, cumin Xinjiang lamb skewers, Grandma's red-braised short ribs. Hong Kong street food energy, curved ceilings, bamboo and a gold birdcage logo. Go in a group, order the lot.
NEW Achara
📍 Aston Quay, Dublin 2 · Northern Thai BBQ · From the Crudo & Hera team
Northern Thai BBQ hitting Dublin — Donegal squid, Leinster lamb ribs, bold Thai technique on Irish produce. Great for groups, exceptional value. Same team as Crudo in Sandymount and Hera in Drumcondra.
NEW ★★ HYPE Allta na Farraige
📍 Grand Canal Dock, Dublin 2 · Seafood & Cocktail Bar · Book ahead
Allta's new seafood bar at Grand Canal Dock. Irish Times called it "the best food I've eaten in Dublin in a while." Lobster and prawn oil in a margarita. If you want the one new-opening booking this month, this is the contender.
