📌 Notes & Logistics
  • Luas & DART normal this weekend — no engineering works confirmed. Green Line to Harcourt for Iveagh Gardens; DART to Dún Laoghaire or Pearse for Trinity.
  • Longitude Festival transport: Avoid driving — Marlay Park parking extremely limited. Bus routes 16/75/116 from city centre, or taxi/rideshare. Allow extra time.
  • Bus diversions from Monday 7 July: Routes 1, 15, 27, 44, 52, C1–C3 divert via D'Olier St due to roadworks. Doesn't affect the weekend but plan your Sunday evening return.

  • Weather tip: Pack a light waterproof jacket — typical Dublin July. Mostly dry but a shower can't be ruled out, especially Saturday morning.
  • Dún Laoghaire Summerfest: Mix of free street events and ticketed venue shows. Check dlrsummerfest.com for full Sunday schedule and advance booking.
