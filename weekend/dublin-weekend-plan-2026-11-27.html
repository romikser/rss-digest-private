🌦️ Weather & getting around

Fri 27 · dry

Met Éireann 05:00 national forecast: bright, mostly sunny, 6–10°C nationally; Friday night cold, later showery rain arriving from the west. Best outdoor photo window today.

Sat 28 · rain passes through

Starts brighter in east; rain/drizzle moves east morning/early afternoon, then brighter; 7–11°C. Put Pearse library or cinema before the fair if rain arrives early.

Sun 29 · brighter, breezy

Heavy showers in east early, clearing to mainly dry bright spells, 8–12°C; windy. Sunday evening turns wet and blustery.

Mon 30 · NOT a holiday

Normal working Monday, not part of this weekend. Wet, windy, locally heavy rain/flooding possible; 10–13°C. Keep the Sunday night journey simple.

🗺️ Three sensible half-days

Friday, sunny and central: Two Pups → Photo Museum before 18:00 → IFI A Woman's Life 20:30. Alternative: Pearse Street small presses → Bolands Mills → IFI 18:15.
Saturday, rain-adaptive: Richmond Barracks fair at 10:00 (mostly sheltered), book a tour only if rain eases → Fumbally lunch → Jimmy Cake 20:00. Fully indoor substitution: Small Press Fair + IFI 14:40 + NCH.
Sunday, slow and musical: Photo Museum at 13:00 → IFI festival closing film 18:00 or an earlier film → DakhaBrakha 19:00 listing. Do not book the 18:00 film and 19:00 gig together; choose one evening anchor.
