Logistics & Notes

Worth Knowing

  • Take That / Aviva Stadium (Sat): Nearest DART stops are Lansdowne Road and Grand Canal Dock — but Grand Canal Dock CLOSES after the concert. Plan your return via Lansdowne Road DART or pre-book a taxi/rideshare ahead of time. Bags strictly A4-size or smaller; no large backpacks.
  • Longitude / Marlay Park (Sat & Sun): No on-site parking; use Luas Green Line to Balally (10-min walk to park) or buses 16/75. Expect heavy crowds from the afternoon. Re-entry policies vary — check the official site. Camping gear not permitted for day tickets.
  • Aviva vs. Longitude clash (Saturday): Both Take That and Longitude run concurrently on Saturday afternoon/evening — decide in advance which event you're attending, as these are on opposite sides of the city.
  • GAA at Croke Park (both days): Camogie All-Ireland Quarter Finals and GAA Hurling All-Ireland Semi-Finals are scheduled this weekend. Expect heavy traffic and bus delays on the northside of the city — avoid the North Circular Road and Jones's Road area around match times.
  • Road closures (Sunday): Burlington Road (D4) and Engine Alley (D8) have planned closures on Sunday 5 July. Allow extra time if driving through those areas.
  • Iveagh Gardens: Small, intimate venue — tickets sell out fast for popular acts. Confirm your tickets are in hand before heading in. Gates typically open 90 minutes before show time.
  • Dún Laoghaire Summerfest: DART to Dún Laoghaire station is the easiest option. Over 50 events across 10 days — check the full programme at dlrsummerfest.com for ticketed evening shows beyond the free street programme.
  • Weather: Classic Irish July — expect anything. A waterproof layer is essential for all outdoor events regardless of the morning forecast.
