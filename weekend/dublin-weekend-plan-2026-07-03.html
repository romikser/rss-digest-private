📋 Notes & Logistics

  • Aviva Stadium (Sat): Zero public parking available. Take the DART to Lansdowne Road — it's right beside the stadium. Alternatively, Dublin Bus 4, 7, 7A, 18, 39A or 46A. Suggested taxi drop: Pembroke Road / Northumberland Road.
  • Marlay Park (Sat & Sun): Allow at least 3 extra hours for travel. Car park tickets via evntz.ie. Best option: coach from Earlsfort Terrace (Marathon Travel) or shuttle bus from Dundrum Luas stop to Stonemasons Way. Bus routes 16 and 74 serve the park (diversions after 10pm).
  • Flavours of Fingal — free entry tip: Entry is completely FREE if you arrive on foot, by bike, or by public transport. Driving? Pre-book discounted parking online. Dublin Airport is 10 minutes away so avoid driving near there mid-day.
  • Longitude Festival + Mumford & Sons clash: Both events use Marlay Park — Longitude runs both Saturday and Sunday. Road pressure around the park will be high all weekend. Plan accordingly if going to both days.
  • Weather prep: Saturday brings a decent chance of light rain/drizzle, especially in the northern parts of the city. Bring a light waterproof. Sunday is warmer and brighter, especially south of the Liffey where Marlay Park and Iveagh Gardens are located.
  • Iveagh Gardens: A compact, beautiful venue — get there early for a good spot. Limited bar queues go fast; bring cash. No large bags.
  • Mumford & Sons — book transport early: Marathon coach services typically sell out for Marlay Park shows. If you haven't already booked a coach, check marathongroup.ie for remaining seats.
