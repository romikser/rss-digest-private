⭐ Editor's picks · ranked

  1. Sun 20:00 · Kronos Quartet plays Steve Reich

    At the NCH: the clearest music-first choice if experimental/minimalism wins over a club night. Ticketed; check seat inventory. NCH Perspectives programme.

  2. Fri 22:00 · Mark Ernestus at Fidelity Studio

    Basic Channel/Rhythm & Sound cofounder, dubwise rather than generic techno. RA lists the event; time reported by a secondary listing, so confirm doors and remaining tickets with the promoter. RA Dublin calendar · Fidelity.

  3. Sat 12:00 · Belle de Jour at IFI

    Introduced Deneuve/Buñuel restoration-era classic; a more distinctive afternoon than multiplex hopping. €15 festival ticket plus €1.50 daily membership if not a member. Official festival booking.

  4. Fri/Sat · IMMA’s new Palestinian-art show

    A Sea for Borrowed Time opened Thursday: photography, moving image, sculpture and political memory, right on the Dublin 8 side. Free, 10:00–17:30 Friday/Saturday; Sunday 12:00–17:30. IMMA exhibition details.

  5. Fri 23:00 · Weval DJ set, Centre Point

    Melodic electronic counterweight to Friday’s harder choices. RA lists €18+ and 01:30 last entry; ticket listing is not a guarantee of remaining capacity. Direct event/tickets.

  6. Sat · Photo Museum’s TALENTS 2026

    Last full Saturday before its 22 Nov close: emerging photography about community and identity. Free; verify current gallery opening hours before heading into Temple Bar. Photo Museum current exhibitions.

  7. Sun 16:30 · Hamaguchi’s All of a Sudden, IFI

    196-minute Japanese-director film in the French festival, unusually good fit for a wet afternoon; leave the evening free. €15 + non-member daily membership. Official Sunday schedule.

🚶 Three workable half-days

1 · Friday, local start and dub finish

Lunch near James’s/Francis Street → IMMA 14:00–16:30 (two distinct exhibitions) → dinner Smithfield/Queen Street → optional quick TwinkleTown pass → Mark Ernestus/Fidelity if tickets and doors check out. All art free; club ticketed.

2 · Saturday, film + photography + rhythm

Temple Bar market while it is dry → Belle de Jour 12:00 → TALENTS 2026 (confirm hours) and Plantation → early dinner → pick Ignez or Klub Sukar, not both. Dalkey print fair is an alternative to this central route.

3 · Sunday rain plan

Skip exposed markets → IFI Donkey Skin 12:00 or All of a Sudden 16:30 (not both if you want a relaxed evening) → nearby food → Kronos Quartet 20:00 only if the film runtime and ticket availability allow it. Alternative: IMMA 12:00–17:30 and an early dinner. Recheck Met and TFI before travelling.
