★ The edit · seven strong picks

Eliza all night at Tengu, Saturday. An all-vinyl arc through electro, acid, EBM and techno, rather than another costume-room playlist. Ticket link; price and remaining inventory not verified. Night bus/taxi home.

Día de Muertos at Button Factory, Sunday 18:00–23:00. Music, dance, food and decorations: a different cultural register from Saturday’s costume carnivals. €25 advertised; check checkout inventory. Venue listing.

The Big Scream on Seán McDermott Street, Saturday 18:00–21:00. Free community carnival; the earlier Diamond Park screenings and Common Street parade make a coherent afternoon. Family-focused, not an underground party. Organiser programme.

🧭 Three half-day routes

Friday: independent film & a small room. Two Pups Francis St in the afternoon → Project Arts Centre Plantation before 18:00 → IFI The Devils 20:40, or Baliza at Whelan’s if live music wins. These last two conflict: choose, don’t sprint. If working Friday, start with the film or gig.
Saturday: close-to-home Halloween. Coffee and daytime Brickfield Park → Digital Hub Depot at 17:30 → cross town for Eliza at Tengu at 23:00, leaving dinner time in between. The first two are family/community events, not rave warmups. For a photo-heavy afternoon instead, swap Brickfield for Fionn McCann at the RHA 11:00–17:00.
Sunday / rain-proof reset. Brunch in Smithfield → IFI Pan’s Labyrinth 13:00 or RHA photo exhibition from noon → Button Factory Día de Muertos 18:00. Most of this route is indoors; the Smithfield-to-Temple-Bar leg needs a jacket, not a marathon crossing.
