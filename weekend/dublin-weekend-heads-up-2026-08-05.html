UK-born, Colombia-based producer Will Holland plays his eclectic blend of funk, soul, nu-jazz and Latin breakbeat. Relevant: he's playing at your bar. Good fit if you want a night out that isn't a standard club.
SAT SUN Howth Roots & Blues Festival
📍 Throughout Howth village · 🕐 From 7 pm evenings, all-day pub trail · FREE (some headline shows ticketed)
40+ free live performances across 7 venues — The Abbey Tavern, The Bloody Stream, The Harbour Bar and more. Blues, roots, Americana, bluegrass. Headliners: Hothouse Flowers, Crow Black Chicken, Rob Strong Band, Zoé Basha. Easy DART from city centre (~30 min), extended late services to 12:25 am. If Sunday clears up as forecast, pair with the cliff walk and harbour seafood.
SAT SUN Dublin Horse Show — RDS
📍 RDS, Ballsbridge · 🕐 All day · Ticketed
Annual show jumping and Irish cultural event running all weekend. World-class equestrian competition plus artisan food, fashion, live entertainment. More of a social occasion than a pure sport spectacle — good option if you want a classic Dublin summer day out with a crowd.

🕐 Sat: 13:05 / 18:50  ·  Sun: 13:25 / 17:50 · 📍 IFI, Temple Bar
