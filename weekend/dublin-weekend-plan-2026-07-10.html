🎬 Cinema This Weekend
MUBI Go Rosebush Pruning
🎭 Karim Aïnouz · 2026 · 97 min · USA/UK/Germany/Italy/Spain · ⭐ Use your MUBI Go ticket this week!
This week’s MUBI release. Aïnouz’s wickedly funny satire of wealth and privilege: four affluent American siblings in a secluded Catalan villa, a secret surrounding their mother’s death, and a cast including Elle Fanning, Pamela Anderson, Jamie Bell, Riley Keough and Callum Turner. Absurdist, sumptuous, and sharp. Showing at both IFI and Lighthouse Cinema this weekend.
IFI Odysseys: Aguirre, the Wrath of God
🕐 Saturday 11 July — 16:00 · 📍 Irish Film Institute, 6 Eustace St, Temple Bar
Werner Herzog’s 1972 masterpiece. Klaus Kinski as the conquistador Aguirre, descending into madness while searching for El Dorado deep in the Peruvian jungle. Part of the IFI’s Odysseys season — an exploration of cinematic journeys. 95 min, subtitled.
IFI Odysseys: Apocalypse Now — The Final Cut
🕐 Sunday 12 July — 15:00 · 📍 Irish Film Institute, Temple Bar
Coppola’s epochal Vietnam War film in its Final Cut form, part of the IFI Odysseys season. One of the great cinema experiences — on the big screen, restored.

Lighthouse Angel’s Egg
🕐 Sunday 12 July · 📍 Light House Cinema, Smithfield · 12A · 71 min
A brand-new 4K restoration of Mamoru Oshii’s haunting 1985 cult anime — in cinemas for the first time. A girl guards a mysterious egg in an underwater city; a soldier arrives searching for a bird from a dream. Visually extraordinary, co-designed with Yoshitaka Amano (Final Fantasy). If you like abstract, meditative anime, this is essential.

Hot Allta na Farraige
📍 One Three Locks Square, Grand Canal Dock, D2 · Seafood & Cocktail Bar · Fri–Sat 13:00–23:00
The year’s most-praised new opening. From Niall Davidson’s Allta team, this cocktail and seafood bar at Grand Canal Dock has been described by Irish Times critics as “equally original and thrilling” and “the best food I’ve eaten in Dublin in a while.” Lobster and prawn oil in a margarita. Open this Saturday — walk-in at the bar; dining room by reservation.
New Achara
📍 Aston Quay, Dublin 2 · Northern Thai BBQ · From the Crudo & Hera team
Northern Thai BBQ lands in Dublin and it’s everything the city needed. Bold Thai technique applied to Irish produce — Donegal squid, Leinster lamb ribs — with premium cocktails. Exceptional value, great for groups.
New Hawker
📍 Rathgar Road, Dublin 6 · Modern Chinese Street Food · From the Hang Dai team
The team behind beloved Hang Dai have opened their second act in Rathmines. Hong Kong-style street food energy: cheeseburger spring rolls with Sichuan ketchup, cumin-spiced Xinjiang lamb skewers, Grandma’s red-braised short ribs. Order everything.
New Kaldero
📍 South King Street, Dublin 2 · Irish-Filipino · Open-fire cooking
Dublin’s first Irish-Filipino restaurant, open-fire cooking, and a first-of-its-kind concept in the city. South King Street is becoming one of Dublin’s best eating streets — Kaldero is the newest reason to go.
Buzz Amai by Viktor
